Philanthropy

Purposeful, structured giving vehicles that align your wealth with your inter-generational legacy.

Designing and Structuring Giving Vehicles

We construct purpose-driven, structured solutions that align your family giving vision with highly efficient legal vehicles:

  • PBO Setup: Full registration and structured setup of Public Benefit Organisations (PBOs).
  • Charitable Trusts: Drafting and establishment of dedicated giving Trusts and Non-Profit Companies (NPCs).
  • Legacy Strategy: Formulating family philanthropy strategies and strong long-term governance guidelines.

Tax Optimization & Statutory Reporting

We take care of the heavy administrative and statutory reporting demands to ensure continuous regulatory compliance:

  • Section 18A: Seamless administration of Section 18A donation certificates and deduction filings.
  • SARS Compliance: Expert SARS PBO registrations and complete statutory submission oversight.
  • Grant Management: Rigorous grant allocation oversight, continuous reporting, and beneficiary vetting.

Generosity achieves the greatest good when backed by solid governance, tax efficiency, and structured execution. Moving from reactive donations to proactive philanthropy requires specialized fiduciary planning.

Our experienced team manages the legal complexities of non-profit operations, ensuring your contributions are fully tax-deductible under South African law and protected for generations.
