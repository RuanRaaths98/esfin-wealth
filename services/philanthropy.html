Philanthropy

Giving made purposeful, structured, and impactful

We help families and businesses establish and administer public benefit organisations (PBOs) and other giving vehicles — ensuring that your philanthropic intentions are realised effectively, compliantly, and with maximum impact.

Book a Consultation All Services
What We Offer

Designing & Administering Giving Structures

Meaningful philanthropy requires more than generosity — it requires the right legal structure, sound governance, and ongoing administration. Our team guides you from the initial vision through registration, grantmaking, and compliance.

  • Registration and setup of public benefit organisations
  • Section 18A donation certificates and compliance
  • Trust and NPC structures for giving
  • SARS registration and ongoing compliance
  • Grant management and reporting
  • Family philanthropy strategy and governance

Why It Matters

Purpose-Driven Wealth

Structured philanthropy allows families to give strategically rather than reactively — ensuring that charitable resources are deployed where they create the greatest impact, while also providing tax efficiency and family legacy.

Tax-Efficient Giving

Section 18A approved structures allow donors to deduct contributions from taxable income.

Legacy and Purpose

We help families articulate a shared philanthropic vision that transcends generations.

Good Governance

Our team ensures your PBO or trust operates compliantly and transparently at all times.
