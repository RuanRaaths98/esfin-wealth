Property Administration

Taking the complexity out of property ownership with seamless tenant placement and robust financial tracking.

Tenant Placement & Lease Administration

We handle every operational detail of your property assets to protect capital values and maintain excellent tenant standards:

  • Placement: Professional tenant sourcing, background checks, and detailed vetting.
  • Lease Management: Precise lease drafting, contract administration, and legal renewals.
  • Collection: Timely monthly rental invoicing, proactive collections, and account reconciliations.

Portfolio Upkeep & Municipal Tracking

We ensure your investment properties remain highly compliant, physically sound, and financially efficient:

  • Maintenance: Seamless coordination of ongoing maintenance, repairs, and vetted contractor administration.
  • Statements: Comprehensive monthly income and expenditure statements for tax filing.
  • Utilities: Proactive tracking of municipal accounts, rates, and utility bills.

Investment property represents a highly profitable asset class, but only when managed with absolute financial and operational discipline. Vacancies and neglected upkeep can quickly erode yields.

Our administration team provides an institutional layer of protection, giving you complete visibility over cash flows, tenant metrics, and portfolio performance without any of the daily friction.
