Structuring

Sophisticated legal and tax structures built to protect corporate and family assets, optimizing efficiency.

Corporate & Holding Structure Design

We establish legally robust and tax-optimized structures that enhance operational flexibility and safeguard your wealth:

  • Company Design: Tailored company formations, group holding setups, and shareholder structures.
  • Trust Interposition: Strategic integration and interposition of family and business trusts.
  • Hybrid Setups: Designing hybrid structures to optimize cross-entity capital flow and compliance.

Corporate Transitions & Tax Optimization

We execute complex reorganizations and cross-border transactions to ensure continuous statutory compliance:

  • Restructuring: Professional advice on Section 42, 44, and 45 corporate restructuring rollovers.
  • Offshore Holdings: Robust offshore structuring, foreign trust integrations, and international holdings.
  • Shareholder Agreements: Secure business buyout structures, funding models, and shareholder agreement reviews.

How you own your assets is just as important as what you own. Without a cohesive, legally sound holding structure, your capital remains exposed to unnecessary taxes, creditor claims, and estate delays.

Our structuring team designs personalized corporate and trust systems that ring-fence your assets from liabilities, streamline inter-generational transfers, and dramatically lower your long-term tax footprint.

Speak with a Structuring Expert
Why It Matters

Structure Is Strategy

How your assets are held is as important as what you own. The correct structure reduces your tax liability today, protects assets from future claims, and ensures that wealth transitions smoothly across generations without triggering unnecessary tax events.

Tax Efficiency

The right holding structure can legally reduce income tax, CGT, and estate duty simultaneously.

Asset Protection

Properly constructed structures ring-fence assets from business and personal liability.

Succession Readiness

Structures designed with succession in mind simplify the transfer of wealth and control.
