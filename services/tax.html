Tax

Proactive tax compliance, strategic planning, and expert SARS representation to protect and optimize your wealth.

Tax Planning & Compliance

We manage your ongoing tax filings with precision, ensuring absolute compliance while identifying strategic avenues for tax optimization:

  • Individual Tax Returns: Precision preparation and submission of annual personal tax returns (ITR12) for high-net-worth families.
  • Corporate Tax Planning: Strategic structuring and filing of corporate tax returns (ITR14) to enhance overall profitability.
  • Provisional Tax Returns: Accurate calculations and timely filing of bi-annual provisional tax returns (IRP6) to prevent penalties.

SARS Disputes & Specialized Audits

We act as your expert representative before SARS, resolving complex audits and securing corporate compliance clearances:

  • SARS Audit Representation: Meticulous audit preparation, compilation of required supporting documents, and professional dispute resolution.
  • CGT & Restructuring: Capital Gains Tax optimization, corporate restructuring rollover relief applications, and asset transfers.
  • Tax Clearance Services: Fast-track processing of Tax Clearance Status (TCS) for foreign investments and good standing.

Tax is one of the single largest friction points in wealth creation. Without continuous, proactive planning, a substantial portion of your annual returns can be lost to avoidable liabilities.

We work closely with you throughout the year, analyzing your investment portfolios, business assets, and trusts to execute legally compliant strategies that lower your effective tax rate and secure your legacy.
