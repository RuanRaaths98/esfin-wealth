Short-Term Insurance

Comprehensive, institutional-grade risk coverage for personal and commercial assets, managed end-to-end.

Personal Asset & Vehicle Protection

We negotiate institutional group rates with leading insurers to ensure your personal holdings are perfectly covered:

  • Homeowners: Premium domestic house and high-value household contents insurance.
  • Vehicles: Highly comprehensive motor vehicle, marine, and corporate fleet coverage.
  • Valuables: Focused, high-value asset cover for jewelry, fine art, and personal technology.

Commercial Premises & Claims Advocacy

We build robust business risk strategies to protect your commercial entities from liability and disruption:

  • Premises: Business property, retail outlets, and commercial asset insurance.
  • Liability: Comprehensive public liability, director’s protection, and professional indemnity.
  • Advocacy: Proactive, complete claims advocacy and structured annual risk exposure reviews.

Being under-insured or caught with restrictive policy exclusions is a significant financial risk. Standard, off-the-shelf retail policies often fail to cover actual replacement costs during major claims.

Our short-term insurance team acts as your dedicated advocate, benchmarking premiums across leading South African underwriters to secure maximum coverage at competitive group rates.

Why It Matters

Being Under-Insured Is a Risk You Cannot Afford

Most policyholders are under-insured without realising it. Our detailed assessment ensures your cover accurately reflects replacement values — so that when you need to claim, your payout covers your actual loss.
