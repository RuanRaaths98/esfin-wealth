Retirement Planning

Integrated wealth accumulation and tax optimization designed to secure a financially independent retirement.

Integrated Pension & Retirement Annuities

We construct comprehensive accumulation strategies that maximize compound growth and minimize tax friction before you retire:

  • Projections: Detailed retirement needs and capital growth projection analysis.
  • Fund Structuring: Structuring corporate pension funds, provident funds, and personal retirement annuities.
  • Drawdowns: Selecting high-efficiency living annuities and guaranteed annuities to secure monthly income.

Employer Advice & Capital Preservation

We coordinate your corporate and personal retirement accounts to prevent tax leakage and inflation erosion:

  • Employer Funds: Specialist advice on employer-sponsored retirement funds and benefit optimization.
  • Pre-Retirement: Strategic pre-retirement tax planning to optimize annual contribution deductions.
  • Drawdowns Strategy: Disciplined post-retirement drawdown schedules to safeguard capital longevity.

Retiring comfortably in South Africa requires planning for longevity, persistent inflation, and complex regulatory frameworks. Relying solely on a basic corporate pension is rarely sufficient.

Our retirement advisory team aligns your investments with Section 28 requirements and custom drawdown profiles, delivering complete tax clearance and steady cash flows for your retirement years.

Why It Matters

The Retirement Planning Gap

Most South Africans retire with insufficient income to maintain their lifestyle. Our disciplined, long-term approach closes that gap — building a strategy that accounts for longevity, inflation, tax, and changing market conditions.

Longevity Planning

We structure income strategies to sustain you through a retirement that may span 30+ years.

Inflation Protection

Your portfolio is designed to generate returns that outpace inflation over the long term.

Tax Efficiency

Maximising retirement contributions and structuring drawdowns reduces your lifetime tax burden.
