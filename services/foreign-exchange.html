Foreign Exchange

Comprehensive currency solutions for every need

Whether you are emigrating, investing offshore, supporting children abroad, or managing cross-border business transactions, our foreign exchange service provides competitive rates, full compliance, and seamless execution.

What We Offer

Currency Solutions & Allowance Administration

South Africa's exchange control regulations are complex and constantly evolving. Our team navigates the rules on your behalf — securing competitive rates, ensuring full SARB compliance, and handling every administrative step from application to transfer.

  • Discretionary and capital allowance management
  • Externalisation of funds for emigration
  • Offshore investment transfers
  • Foreign maintenance and education allowances
  • Business and trade payment facilitation
  • SARB and SARS compliance and reporting
