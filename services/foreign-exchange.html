Foreign Exchange

Highly competitive institutional rates, complete exchange control compliance, and seamless cross-border transfers.

Currency Solutions & Capital Transfers

We negotiate institutional rates that consistently outperform standard retail bank margins, ensuring your funds transfer smoothly and cost-effectively:

  • Allowances: Complete discretionary and capital allowance facilitation up to statutory limits.
  • Fund Externalisation: Seamless externalisation of funds for emigration or relocation purposes.
  • Offshore Investing: Direct transfer support for offshore portfolios and global asset acquisition.

SARB Reporting & Trade Facilitation

We navigate complex and evolving South African exchange control regulations on your behalf:

  • Maintenance & Education: Hassle-free foreign maintenance and international education payment administration.
  • Business Trade: Professional business payment facilitation and commercial cross-border trade transactions.
  • SARB Compliance: Thorough SARS Tax Clearance Application management and complete SARB reporting compliance.

Exchange control non-compliance carries severe legal and financial penalties. Our specialists take full charge of the administrative process, ensuring every transfer is correctly documented and approved.

We combine institutional-grade execution speed with curated, competitive pricing, delivering an end-to-end foreign exchange service that brings ultimate efficiency to your global capital flows.
