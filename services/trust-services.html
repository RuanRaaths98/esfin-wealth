Trust Services

Establishing and administering trusts with precision

From initial formation through to ongoing administration and eventual dissolution, our trust services team ensures your trusts are properly established, compliantly maintained, and actively managed in the interests of all beneficiaries.

Book a Consultation All Services
What We Offer

Local & Offshore Trust Administration

A trust is only as effective as its administration. We provide end-to-end trust services — from drafting the founding deed and registering with SARS, through to annual compliance, trustee meetings, and beneficiary distributions — ensuring your trust fulfils its intended purpose at every stage.

  • Trust deed drafting and amendment
  • Trust registration with the Master of the Court
  • SARS registration and income tax returns
  • Annual trustee meetings and minutes
  • Trust bookkeeping and financial statements
  • Offshore trust formation and administration

Why It Matters

The Right Administration Makes All the Difference

A poorly administered trust can be challenged, set aside, or found non-compliant — nullifying years of careful planning. Our team ensures that every procedural and regulatory requirement is met, preserving the integrity and effectiveness of your trust at all times.

Regulatory Compliance

We ensure full compliance with the Trust Property Control Act and all SARS requirements.

Offshore Structures

Our team facilitates formation and administration of trusts in leading offshore jurisdictions.

Beneficiary Reporting

We provide clear, regular reporting to trustees and beneficiaries on trust activities and performance.
