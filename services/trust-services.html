Trust Services

Expert trust establishment, comprehensive local and offshore trust administration, and full statutory compliance to safeguard your family legacy.

Trust Formation & Setup

We establish legally robust trust structures designed to protect assets and facilitate smooth wealth transfer:

  • Deed Drafting: Strategic drafting and customization of trust deeds to align with your family governance and wealth objectives.
  • Court Registrations: Secure and prompt registration of family and business trusts with the Master of the High Court.
  • Trustee Advisory Setup: Expert onboarding and structuring of trustee boards, letters of authority, and administrative protocols.

Trust Administration & Compliance

We maintain the operational and legal integrity of your trust with rigorous compliance and administrative services:

  • SARS Registrations: Fast-track trust registration with SARS for Income Tax, provisional tax, and VAT compliance.
  • Annual Tax Returns: Preparation and submission of annual trust income tax returns (IT12TR) in absolute alignment with tax laws.
  • Bookkeeping & Registry: Comprehensive trust bookkeeping, financial statements preparation, and trust asset registry tracking.

A trust is one of the most powerful structures for asset protection and succession planning, but it is only as strong as its administration. Poorly administered trusts risk being deemed sham trusts or non-compliant by SARS.

Our specialized trust services team ensures that all resolutions, financial records, and statutory submissions are immaculately maintained, fully protecting the trust's legal validity and the beneficiaries' interests.

Why It Matters

The Right Administration Makes All the Difference

A poorly administered trust can be challenged, set aside, or found non-compliant — nullifying years of careful planning. Our team ensures that every procedural and regulatory requirement is met, preserving the integrity and effectiveness of your trust at all times.

Regulatory Compliance

We ensure full compliance with the Trust Property Control Act and all SARS requirements.

Offshore Structures

Our team facilitates formation and administration of trusts in leading offshore jurisdictions.

Beneficiary Reporting

We provide clear, regular reporting to trustees and beneficiaries on trust activities and performance.
