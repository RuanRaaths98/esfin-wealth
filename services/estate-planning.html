Estate Planning

Protecting your legacy across every generation with comprehensive wills and inheritance structures.

Protect your legacy across every generation

We design bespoke legacy strategies to ensure your wealth transfers smoothly, tax-efficiently, and precisely as you intend:

  • Wills: Drafting and reviewing last will and testament.
  • Estate Structuring: Estate duty and CGT planning to minimise tax leakage.
  • Wealth Transfer: Strategic inter-generational wealth transfer strategies.

Continuous Compliance & Support

We take the complexity out of trust structures and estate execution, safeguarding vulnerable beneficiaries:

  • Fiduciary Appointments: Professional nomination of executors and trustees.
  • Guardianship: Clear letters of wishes and family guardianship planning.
  • Business Succession: Arranging structured transitions and continuity plans for business entities.

Effective estate planning is about more than writing a will. We design robust structures that minimise estate duty, avoid unnecessary delays, and ensure your beneficiaries receive exactly what you intended.

We work closely with you to understand your specific family dynamics, domestic or offshore asset structures, and long-term legacy goals, bringing ultimate certainty and peace of mind.

Speak with an Estate Planning Expert
Why It Matters

Why Estate Planning Cannot Wait

Dying intestate — without a valid, up-to-date will — can trigger costly legal delays and leave your estate vulnerable to taxes and disputes. A well-structured estate plan gives you control and gives your family certainty.

Estate Duty Minimisation

Strategic structuring can significantly reduce the estate duty payable on your death.

Seamless Succession

A clear succession plan avoids disputes and ensures business continuity.

Beneficiary Protection

Trusts and other structures protect assets for minor or vulnerable beneficiaries.
