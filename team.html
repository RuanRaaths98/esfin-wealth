The People Behind Esfin

Three decades of trusted service, carried forward by a team spanning accounting, administration, and wealth planning — and the next generation preparing to lead it.

Elize Symington

Owner & Founder / Accountant

elizesym@esfin.co.za

As Owner and Founder of Esfin, Elize Symington has spent the past 30 years building the practice from the ground up into the trusted, multidisciplinary firm it is today. A qualified Accountant, she has personally guided generations of clients through their accounting, tax, and financial affairs, building relationships grounded in integrity, discretion, and genuine care.

Her hands-on leadership and deep technical knowledge continue to shape the standards and culture of the practice. As Esfin transitions into its next generation, Elize remains actively involved, ensuring that the values and personal service on which the firm was built carry through for many years to come.

As Esfin moves into its next generation, Elize remains a key member of the team, helping to ensure that the firm continues to operate efficiently while maintaining the high standards of accuracy, professionalism and service that our clients have come to expect.
