Taxation Services

Proactive tax planning, compliance, and expert opinions

We provide advice, planning, structuring, returns, compliance and formal opinions — helping you stay compliant while minimizing your tax burden.

  • Planning: Proactive tax planning tailored to your personal and business circumstances.

  • Structuring: Tax-efficient structuring recommendations for individuals and businesses.

Secretarial Services

Complete company and statutory secretarial support

We manage the statutory and regulatory administration that keeps your company or trust in good standing, from CIPC registrations to Master of the High Court compliance.

  • CIPC Registrations: Company and close corporation registrations with the CIPC.
  • CIPC Compliance: Ongoing CIPC compliance, annual returns, and amendments.
  • Master's Office Registrations: Registration of trusts and estates with the Master of the High Court.
  • Master's Office Compliance: Ongoing compliance for trusts and deceased estates.
